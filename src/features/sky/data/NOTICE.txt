Sky chart data — third-party notice

stars.json and constellation-lines.json are derived from d3-celestial by Olaf Frohn
(https://github.com/ofrohn/d3-celestial), whose star data is based on XHIP
(Anderson & Francis 2012, VizieR V/137D) and whose constellation figures are the
author's adaptation of IAU/Stellarium line data. Regenerate with scripts/build-sky-data.mjs.
